Most icons in this folder are from **Mulberry Symbols** by Steve Lee, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://mulberrysymbols.org/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

CC BY-SA 4.0 requires that anything you publish with these icons credits Mulberry Symbols and Steve Lee, links to the license, and says if you changed them. Changed versions of the icons must be shared under the same license.

`icons.json` lists the Mulberry symbol each word uses in its `mulberry` field. For words where `match` is "Related / partial", the symbol shows a related or narrower meaning (for example `kirja` uses Mulberry's "work_book"); the `note` field explains.

These three images are **not** Mulberry symbols. They are custom companion artwork, and their license is not stated here:

- `tuuli.webp` (wind)
- `lapsi.webp` (child)
- `nena.webp` (nose)

The files are unchanged from the source collection. Their `box` values in `icons.json` are measurements only.
